      Statutory provisions & citizen safeguards:

      RTI Act, 2005 Core Safeguards

      Section 6(3): Mandatory Transfer
      If an application is made to a public authority not holding the information, they must transfer it to the concerned authority within 5 days and notify you.
      Section 7(1): 30-Day Mandatory Clock
      The PIO must provide the records within 30 days. If the information concerns the Life or Liberty of a person, it must be provided within 48 hours.
      Section 19(1): First Appeal
      If no reply is received within 30 days or if you are aggrieved by the decision, you can file a First Appeal within 30 days to the First Appellate Authority (FAA) at zero fee.
      Section 20: Penalty on PIO
      The Information Commission can impose a fine of ₹250 per day (up to ₹25,000) on the PIO for unreasonable delays or refusing applications.

      Police Encounters & Arrests

      Zero FIR Right
      A Zero FIR can be lodged at any police station regardless of jurisdiction. It is then transferred to the appropriate police station.
      24-Hour Production Before Magistrate
      Under Article 22(2) of the Constitution, every arrested person must be presented before a Judicial Magistrate within 24 hours.
      Arrest of Women (Sunset to Sunrise)
      Women cannot be arrested between sunset and sunrise except under exceptional circumstances with prior magistrate permission.

      Consumer Protection Act, 2019

      Deficiency of Service & e-Daakhil
      Consumers can file claims up to ₹50 Lakhs in the District Consumer Commission directly via edaakhil.nic.in.
